The four principles

Lighting · Sightlines · Planting

Natural surveillance

People behave differently when they could be seen. Light, windows and clear lines of sight put eyes on the street without a single camera.

Defined space

Access control

Paths, gateways and landscaping guide people where you want them to go, and make it obvious when someone is somewhere they shouldn’t be.

Defined space

Territorial reinforcement

Clear boundaries and a sense of ownership tell everyone this place belongs to someone, and that they’re looking after it.

Maintenance

Maintenance

Broken glass and graffiti signal that nobody is watching. Quick repairs signal the opposite, and stop the slide.
