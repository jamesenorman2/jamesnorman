The four principles

Lighting · Frontages · Planting

Natural surveillance

Arrange a place so people can be seen going about it: light, windows and clear lines of sight put eyes on the street without a single camera.

Gate the alley

Natural access control

Paths, gates and landscaping guide people where you want them, and make it obvious when someone is somewhere they shouldn’t be.

Gate the alley

Territorial reinforcement

Clear boundaries between public, shared and private space tell everyone a place belongs to someone.

Maintenance

Maintenance

Broken lights and graffiti signal that nobody is watching. Quick repairs signal the opposite, and keep the other measures working.
