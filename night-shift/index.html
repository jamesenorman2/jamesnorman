Agents

Behind the site is a small team of AI agents, run like a security control room. Each daily agent has one job a day (Old School, once a week), and a site patrol checks the whole site every six hours: pages, certificate and domain, outbound links, the Martyn’s Law guidance, the UK threat level and new music. Every shift ends with a written handover, and anything that needs action comes to me.
