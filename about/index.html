About

James Norman

Security consultant by day, producer and DJ the rest of the time. This site is where both live, and a few things in between.

The work

Protective security

As a technical director for AECOM, Chartered Security Consultant and security leader with over 27 years of experience, I specialise in protecting people, assets, and operations across critical national infrastructure and large-scale global capital projects.

My approach is grounded in pragmatic problem-solving and risk-based engineering. I help clients navigate complex security requirements, translating evolving threat pictures into solutions that are proportionate, robust, and directly aligned with their operational objectives. Whether addressing physical and electronic security or complex counter-terrorism strategies, the goal is always to deliver guidance that is workable and right-sized to the risk.

Leading security consultancy across the UK and the wider AMEA region, my portfolio spans city-level masterplanning, aviation, energy, data centres, sporting arenas, and CNI sites. The constant across all of this work is an in-depth understanding of the threat landscape and the engineering judgement required to safeguard against it.

  • BEng (Hons) Electrical & Electronic Engineering
  • CEng Chartered Engineer (IET)
  • Principal Member, Register of Security Engineers and Specialists (RSES), sponsored by the National Protective Security Authority (NPSA)
  • MIET Member of the Institution of Engineering and Technology
  • BRE SABRE Accredited Assessor and Registered Professional
  • SQSS-compliant consultant for HEA 06 Security (BREEAM)
